Channel Coach privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Channel Coach is a personal tool that Johnson Ocheja uses with his own YouTube channels. It runs as an extension inside the Claude desktop app on his computer. This page explains what it does with data from Google and YouTube.

What Google data it uses

When a channel is connected, Google asks for three read-only permissions: to see the YouTube account, to view YouTube Analytics reports, and to view monetary YouTube Analytics reports. Channel Coach uses them to read the connected channel's details, its videos and its statistics, including views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, audience age, gender and country, and estimated revenue. It also reads public YouTube data, such as other channels' videos, view counts and public comments.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the channel owner's own questions inside Claude, for example how a video is performing or which topics are working for similar channels. Channel Coach can't upload, change or delete anything on YouTube.

Where the data is stored

The sign-in token for each connected channel is saved in a private folder on the owner's computer. Statistics are fetched from YouTube when a question needs them, and Channel Coach does not save them. It also keeps a count of its YouTube requests for the day on the same computer, so it can stay within YouTube's daily limits.

Who can see the data

Results are passed to Claude, the AI assistant made by Anthropic, as part of the owner's own conversations, where they are handled under the owner's Claude account and Anthropic's privacy policy. Channel Coach does not sell the data, share it with anyone else or use it for advertising. This website has no analytics or cookies.

Google and YouTube policies

Channel Coach uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your information under the Google Privacy Policy. Channel Coach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Channel Coach's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. Disconnecting a channel inside Channel Coach also cancels its sign-in and deletes the saved token from the computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Channel Coach.
