Channel Coach

A personal tool that lets Claude read the YouTube Analytics of my own channels, so it can help me plan and improve my videos.

I make videos about contemporary art across several YouTube channels. Channel Coach runs on my computer as an extension for the Claude desktop app. When I ask Claude how a video is doing or what to make next, Channel Coach fetches the numbers from YouTube so the answer is based on my real data.

It is not a public service. It only connects to channels I own, and I am its only user.

What it reads

For my connected channels, it reads views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, audience age, gender and country, and estimated revenue. It also looks up public YouTube information, such as other channels' videos and view counts, so I can compare my channels with similar ones.

The Google permissions it asks for

Google shows three read-only permissions when a channel is connected:

  • See your YouTube account, to know which channel was connected and list its videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports, for views, watch time, retention, traffic and audience.
  • View monetary YouTube Analytics reports, for estimated revenue on monetized channels.

Channel Coach can't upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

How the data is stored and used is explained in the Channel Coach privacy policy.
